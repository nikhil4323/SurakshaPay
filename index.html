    Three Golden Rules for Safe Digital Payments

    Essential precautions for senior citizens, parents, small merchants, and first-time digital payment users.

    Rule 01
    No PIN Needed to Receive Money

    UPI PIN is ONLY typed when SENDING money, NEVER when RECEIVING money. Anyone asking for your PIN to send you cash is attempting fraud.

    Rule 02
    Never Install Apps from Calls or Links

    No legitimate bank, utility company, or government department will ever ask you to download AnyDesk, QuickSupport, or .APK files over phone or SMS.

    Rule 03
    When in Doubt, Pause and Verify

    If you feel pressured, frightened, or confused, immediately hang up and talk to a trusted family member or visit your local bank branch.
